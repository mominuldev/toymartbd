# Channel logos

`whatsapp.svg` and `messenger.svg` are the WhatsApp and Messenger paths from Simple Icons
(https://simpleicons.org, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), released under CC0 1.0,
filled with each brand's own colour (#25D366, #00B2FF). They are the same marks as the Figma
`BrandLogo` components `logo/whatsapp` 613:2705 and `logo/messenger` 613:2708.

The marks remain trademarks of WhatsApp LLC and Meta Platforms, Inc. They are shown only to
identify the channel on Inbox › Channels.
