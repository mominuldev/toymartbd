# Payment gateway logos

`sslcommerz.svg` and `paystation.svg` are exported from the approved Figma file
(`PWZp5VzEgEEf5N2Y38lGIh`), components `logo/sslcommerz` (613:2694) and `logo/paystation`
(613:2699), on 2026-11-20. The only change after export is removing the Figma page's own
background rectangles that the export wraps around a component; the marks are unchanged.
`paystation.svg` embeds the mark as a PNG, as it is in Figma.

The SSLCommerz and PayStation marks are trademarks of their respective owners. They are not covered by this project's licence and
are shown only to identify each payment gateway on Integrations › Payments and the
Integrations hub — nominative use, never as our own branding.

Where Figma got the marks from is not recorded in this repository. Before launch, check each
against the gateway's published brand assets and its merchant terms on logo use.
