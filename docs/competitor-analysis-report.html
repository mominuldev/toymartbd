01

Method and evidence discipline

Homepage, a category listing, a product page, cart, checkout, sitemap.xml, robots.txt and a blog page were fetched per site with a desktop Chrome user-agent, redirects followed. Raw HTML and JavaScript bundles were read directly rather than converted, so platform fingerprints, JSON-LD and meta tags stay quotable. Cart and checkout were inspected with real cart sessions, because all three stores in one batch hide checkout behind an empty-cart redirect. No orders were placed.

Yes

Observed in a fetched response.

No

Verified absent. Searched for in the place it would have to appear, and not found.

NV

Not verified. Client-rendered, gated or unreachable. Not the same as absent.

Stated limitations

Two sites could not be fully audited because their money pages render entirely client-side. Their rows are NV, not No — though that opacity is itself a finding. sktoy.com.bd and krystal.com.bd serve a Cloudflare-managed robots.txt containing User-agent: ClaudeBot / Disallow: /; fetch volume was kept low and user-directed, and the restriction is recorded rather than worked around. Catalogue sizes are counted from sitemaps and are therefore a floor, not an exact figure.

02

The headline

This market is larger in count than in strength.

1customer review across roughly 1,100 products in the five-site batch
0of 8 niche stores offer a brand filter or a gender filter
0of 11 properties offer gift wrap, a gift message or a gift finder
6of 8 real competitors run WooCommerce, directly or headless
0have a purpose-built operational back end

Among the eight real competitors: a store with 820 products that emits no structured data at all — no canonical, no Open Graph, no JSON-LD, on any page type. A COD-only store that collects no phone number, making its orders unfulfillable by any Bangladeshi courier. A store whose only payment method is asking a stranger to transfer money first and then type their own account number and transaction ID. A store that returns one byte-identical page for every URL, including ones that do not exist. And a single customer review across roughly eleven hundred products.

None of this is a hard problem. It is an unattended one. The opening is not a feature — it is competence applied in two places at once: a storefront that is simply correct and fast, and a back office that tells the owner the truth about money.

04

Four universal gaps

Absent across every auditable competitor. Each is cheap to build, and none requires beating anyone on price or catalogue size.

01

Nobody lets a parent filter by age

Two sites navigate by age; only EduToyZone makes it a filter. None lets you combine age with price, brand or gender. Even Daraz stores Recommended Age as a seller-typed free-text attribute, producing about thirty overlapping, unsortable buckets on one page — “3 years”, “From 0 month +”, “0 - 12 years”, “10 and Up”.

A single-merchant store with age stored in months beats the market leader on its own best facet.

02

Nobody has reviews

One real review across roughly 1,100 products. Eight of nine emit no AggregateRating — and neither marketplace emits it on its product pages either.

Review stars in Google results are unclaimed by the entire category.

03

Nobody has working content

Every blog is empty, dormant or demo filler. Kids Republic still serves 2019 theme placeholder posts — and lists them in its production sitemap. Baby Bazar serves live SEO-spam pages on a children's store (“What Is an Online Slot Machine?”, “Cheap Essays…”), both HTTP 200 and in the sitemap.

All parenting, age-guide and gift-guide intent is uncontested. Three sites additionally block AI crawlers, conceding AI-answer citations too.

04

Nobody's data is trustworthy

Products priced ৳0 marked InStock. Three contradictory free-delivery thresholds on one site — on two separate sites. A live page printing its own hotline as xxxx-xxxxx, twice. 19 of 28 homepage products out of stock. A working order-tracking page wrapped in unedited theme demo content: lorem ipsum, a Brooklyn NY address and “UTC +2” opening hours.

Being merely accurate is a differentiator here.

Site dossiers

Strengths and observed defects per site. Everything below was seen in a fetched response.

01  SK Toy

Next.js App Router → Node/Mongo API · 81 products · TTFB 1.52s

Strengths

  • The only site with a complete paid-acquisition stack: GA4 G-R9C6P60JKN, Meta Pixel 1516765536845697, and uniquely a TikTok pixel.
  • Heavy Bangla content. Age-tile browsing. Working /track page. Published delivery charges (৳80 / ৳130).
  • COD enabled; three-level category depth.

Observed defects

  • Critical The product page's <main> contains only a spinning loader. The homepage is server-rendered; every listing and product page is not.
  • Product schema is broken: "price": 0 alongside "availability": "InStock", HTML inside description, and a shippingRate of 60 that contradicts its own ৳80/৳130 config.
  • Three different free-delivery thresholds — ৳3,000 in the banner, ৳2,500 in config, a flat ৳60 in schema.
  • Two products priced ৳0, four “Coming Soon”, one “SOLD OUT” on the homepage. bKash badges in the footer while the gateway is enabled:false.

Verdict: the best-instrumented competitor has excluded its own product and category pages from organic search. Those queries are free.

02  Krystal

Astro + headless WooCommerce · 820 products · 17.9 KB gzipped

Strengths

  • Best checkout in the set: one page, seven fields, 64 districts with dependent thana selects, email optional, COD and SSLCommerz, coupon field, and charges stated in Bangla (Dhaka ৳70 / nationwide ৳115, free over ৳1,500).
  • The only competitor with a working automated gateway. The only one with an AI chat assistant. Names four couriers. Shows low-stock counts. Has Messenger.

Observed defects

  • Critical 820 products and zero JSON-LD, zero canonical, zero Open Graph on every page type. The single biggest SEO hole found anywhere in this audit.
  • /cart/ returns 404 — there is no cart page.
  • Shipping page says free delivery over ৳10,000; checkout says ৳1,500.
  • The hotline prints as xxxx-xxxxx twice on a live page. One courier is misspelled “SteadTas”. Flat, single-level category structure. Sort options ship disabled. 88 blog posts, all off-market filler.

Verdict: the strongest commerce mechanics in the market, attached to no discoverability at all. Its checkout is the bar to match.

03  Toyshop

WordPress + WooCommerce + Elementor · 126 products · 90 KB gzipped / 421 KB raw

Strengths

  • Fastest measured TTFB in the set at 0.48s.
  • Server-rendered throughout. Working /track-order/. AJAX search autocomplete. Price filter present.

Observed defects

  • Critical No COD. The only gateways are woo_bkash, woo_rocket and woo_nagad, all manual transfer: “Bkash করুন এই নাম্বারেঃ 01941111000… You need to send us ৳3350.00”, after which the buyer types their own account number and transaction ID.
  • Three-step checkout, required email, a 2 + 2 = ? math captcha, and untranslated WooCommerce labels (“State / County”, “Postcode / ZIP”).
  • Delivery charge invisible until an address is entered: “Enter your address to view shipping options”.
  • 19 of 28 homepage products out of stock; most listing buttons read “Read more” instead of “Add to cart”. 11 <h1> elements on the homepage. No meta descriptions. No Meta Pixel. Wishlist is CSS only, with no button.

Verdict: a stranger is asked to send money before an order exists. This is the clearest demonstration in the audit that the market's problem is execution, not technology.

04  Kids Republic

WordPress 7.1.4 + WooCommerce 11.2.2 + Yoast · 71 products · TTFB 2.27s

Strengths

  • The only site in the audit with AggregateRating in its schema — and the only one with a real customer review.
  • Strongest structured data overall (Yoast): Product, Offer, BreadcrumbList, Organization. Meta descriptions present. Age taxonomy. Variants. Wishlist.

Observed defects

  • Critical 91 <script> tags and 21 stylesheets on a single product page, with the slowest TTFB measured at 2.27s.
  • Its AggregateRating is built on one review.
  • Duplicate rel=canonical emitted on the same page.
  • Still serving 2019 theme demo posts — /2019/03/27/10-student-discount/ renders as “Grow / Post By admin / Slider” — and listing them in its sitemap.
  • Order tracking page 404s. No delivery charge published anywhere. No Meta Pixel. viewport declared without initial-scale.

Verdict: the best SEO foundations in the market, undermined by page weight and three years of unattended content.

05  Kids Zone BD

Angular SPA on saleecom · catalogue size unknown · ~682 KB JS before first paint

Strengths

  • Fast shell delivery (0.19s) and the smallest HTML payload measured — because it contains almost nothing.
  • Wishlist and product compare are present in the bundle.

Observed defects

  • Critical Returns one byte-identical 53,313-byte shell titled <title>E-commerce Platform</title> for every URL — including /this-path-does-not-exist-12345. All responses hash to the same MD5.
  • No sitemap (/sitemap.xml serves the shell), no canonical, no Open Graph, no JSON-LD.
  • No analytics or pixels of any kind. A branded search for the domain surfaced nothing from it.
  • Blocks pinch-zoom (user-scalable=no, maximum-scale=1.0). PWA scaffolding is commented out, so there is no favicon and no manifest. Dead third-party CDN assets still referenced. cache-control: no-store on the one cacheable asset.

Verdict: not competing for organic traffic, and not measuring anything. Effectively absent.

06  Toy House BD

WooCommerce + Elementor / ShopEngine · 12 products

Strengths

  • Server-rendered. Guest checkout. Price filter, wishlist and compare present.

Observed defects

  • Critical No phone field at checkout at all — billing_phone appears only in a WooCommerce i18n config object — while the store is COD-only. These orders cannot be fulfilled by any Bangladeshi courier.
  • Critical No shipping is ever calculated. Order total equals subtotal, despite a “Flat-rate Delivery” badge.
  • 12 products total; 4 of its 11 menu categories contain none. No Product JSON-LD (ShopEngine bypasses WooCommerce's schema). No meta descriptions. Zero analytics or pixels. No Bangla.
  • About twelve unconfigured theme-demo pages live in the sitemap (/shop-2/, /checkout-2/, /manage-profile-3/). Product copy reads “1 pen is added”.

Verdict: a soft-launch shell. Not currently a commercial threat.

07  Baby Bazar BD

WooCommerce 11.2.2 + Woodmart + Yoast · 666 products · 80 categories, 3 levels

Strengths

  • The best taxonomy in the market: shop-by-age plus a ten-goal therapeutic skill axis (fine motor, sensory processing, bilateral integration…) and a special-needs category. Genuinely differentiated.
  • Real Bangla h1 and category tiles. Names both Pathao and eCourier. ECAB and DBID trust badges. iOS and Android apps. “Tika Reminder” and “Hello Doctor” services. Complete Open Graph. Meta Pixel 229240508811973.

Observed defects

  • Critical Zero filters on 666 products. The sidebar is CSS-hidden and empty — no price, age, brand or gender filter.
  • No stock status anywhere, including on variable products, and no max on quantity inputs.
  • The homepage is an 80-item alphabetical catalogue dump (578 KB) that opens with cotton buds. Checkout inlines the entire terms and conditions, producing a 325 KB page.
  • Live SEO-spam pages on a children's store — “What Is an Online Slot Machine?”, “Cheap Essays…” — both HTTP 200 and in the sitemap.
  • /track-order/ works but is wrapped in unedited theme demo content: lorem ipsum, a Brooklyn NY address, “UTC +2” hours. No reviews. Flat ৳130 national rate from a Chattogram base. No GA4 or GTM. Flash-sale capability present but explicitly disabled (sale_countdown:0).

Verdict: the right taxonomy with none of the merchandising to exploit it. Its skill axis is the idea worth learning from.

08  EduToyZone Benchmark

nopCommerce with purpose-built plugins · 510 products

Strengths

  • Not a stock theme — custom plugins (ETZ.Plugin.Misc.ProductDiscovery, ETZ.Plugin.Misc.OneCspCheckout).
  • The only real merchandising filters in the market: recommended age (7 bands, with result counts), learning goal, price bands, in-stock only. Age-first and skill-first three-level navigation.
  • Bundles and combo offers. Variants with per-variant pricing and per-variant stock. Product video. Reviews. Free-delivery progress bar.
  • Best-localised checkout found: phone-first, email optional, district select with Dhaka-subcity granularity, and genuine three-tier zone pricing — inside Dhaka ৳70, Dhaka subcity ৳100, outside Dhaka ৳120.
  • Guest order lookup at /findorder (order ID + last four phone digits). WhatsApp and Messenger in three places. Extensive Bangla.
  • Best SEO in the set: Product + Offer (BDT) + BreadcrumbList + per-product FAQPage in Bangla. The only full analytics stack: GTM-W7DGWV43, G-ZSFE8WM8TS, Pixel 1071219174683903.

Observed defects — where it is still beatable

  • Free-delivery threshold contradicts itself three ways — ৳1,999 / ৳2,000 / ৳3,000, sometimes on the same page.
  • The product-page age badge says “Age 2 years+” while Specifications says “3/4/5 Year +” on the same product.
  • Filters are client-side with no pushState, so a filtered view cannot be shared, bookmarked or indexed. The strongest feature in the market generates no landing pages.
  • No brand filter, no gender filter, no flash sale or countdown. Reviews are very sparse and there is no AggregateRating. No Open Graph on the homepage. Blog page has no posts.

Verdict: the site to beat, and beatable on specifics rather than on strategy. Its operational back end is a generic .NET admin, not a Bangladesh-specific order, courier, risk and P&L system.

09  kidszone.com Not a competitor

No platform · no catalogue · no <title>

What it actually is

  • 27 KB of HTML with no <title>, no products, no navigation, no prices and no commerce markup. No WordPress, Shopify, Laravel or Next.js markers — no platform at all.
  • The body is a consentmanager.net GDPR/CCPA script supporting 32 European languages plus US state-privacy keys, and one parked-domain ad pixel: l.cdn-fileserver.com/bping.php?…vgd_*&prid=…&crid=….
  • /robots.txt connection-resets. /sitemap.xml returns an HTML error page, not XML.

Why it looked Bangladeshi

  • The tracking pixel carries cc=BD, which reflects the geolocation of the requesting IP address — not the site's market. The GDPR-global default, European language list and US state-privacy handling all point to a US or EU domain-monetisation operator.

Action: remove from the competitor set. Any brief listing it as a Bangladeshi toy retailer is mistaken.

07

AppByte benchmark

Benchmarked as a software product, not a competing store — it sells the back office this project also needs.

There is no product site to audit

appbyte.net serves EcomByte by appByte Technologies Ltd. It is a fully client-rendered single-page React application. Sixteen paths were probed — robots.txt, sitemap.xml, /features, /pricing, /docs, /blog, /demo, /integrations, /changelog, /api-docs, /login, /dashboard and others — and every one returns the identical 634,483-byte shell. The footer lists all of those pages; the links have no href. There is no documentation, help centre, API reference, changelog, legal page or demo tenant.

Everything below was recovered by extracting string literals from the inlined JavaScript bundle: a 29-item FAQ array, a three-plan pricing array, a 31-row comparison table and a 29-card feature grid. That is reliable evidence of what AppByte claims, and nothing more. No independent verification exists — no review, comparison, testimonial or forum thread in English or Bengali. Every screenshot on the site is a hand-coded mock with fabricated data.

Claimed modules

Orders with six statuses, phone as an order source, and ad click-IDs retained 90 days · inventory with batch tracking and multi-warehouse · four couriers · Fake Order Guard · accounting including projected profit from in-transit parcels · COD amounts due per courier · invoices with 10+ templates and barcode stickers · CRM with OTP login and one-click WhatsApp · coupons, bundles and B2B quotations · role-based access · Bengali SMS and browser push · Meta CAPI and TikTok Events · a landing-page builder.

Pricing is annual prepay, not monthly SaaS: ৳7,000, ৳12,000 and ৳30,000 per year, hosting bundled, no commission. Every call to action is “contact us” via WhatsApp — there is no signup, trial or checkout. For context, Easy Order Manager is ৳390/month and Bonik AI around ৳590/month; the market clears near ৳400–1,000 per month.

One internal contradiction worth noting: the pricing table claims data from ৭ টি কুরিয়ার (seven couriers) while the FAQ says four and the interface renders an array of length four.

The fake-order check, and why we will build it differently

Lookup is by phone number, returning per-courier signals beside each courier's logo: parcel and delivery counts, success ratio, number age, address verification, “4 names at one address”, “3 COD payments not taken”. Three tiers map to auto-confirm, phone-verify and block, with an explicit stated rule: স্কোর ৭০% ছাড়ালে অর্ডার নিজে থেকেই আটকে যায় — above a 70% risk score the order is held automatically.

AppByte never discloses its data source. The category mechanism was confirmed from working open-source code: vendors store the merchant's courier-portal email and password and call reverse-engineered internal endpoints on Pathao, Steadfast, RedX and Carrybee. Undocumented, unsupported, and requiring stored passwords.

Toy Mart BD will not do that. Steadfast publishes an official GET /fraud_check/{phone} endpoint in its own Postman collection. We use that plus our own order history, and accept thinner signal on a genuinely first-time customer as the price of not holding credentials we have no right to. We agree with the auto-hold; we will never auto-cancel, because a false positive on a real parent costs a customer and a public review, while a held order costs one phone call.

What AppByte appears not to do

No supplier or purchase-order module at all · no landed costing · no partial-delivery state, although both couriers support it · no COD settlement-to-consignment matching · no COD gating by success rate · no VAT/Mushak-compliant invoicing · and OTP and SMS are paywalled out of the cheapest tier, which is the tier its fraud-check marketing targets. It also does not include a storefront.

08

Marketplace baseline

Daraz and Othoba set what Bangladeshi shoppers expect. A niche store cannot match their price, breadth or voucher budget — so what matters is where they are structurally weak.

Daraz — toys

4,080 results across 15–16 facets, and the richest promotional mechanics observed anywhere in this study: copy-to-clipboard vouchers, collectible vouchers, live and pre-launch countdowns, multi-buy, combos, freebies and loyalty coins.

Where it is weak

  • Only three sort options across 4,080 products — best match and two price directions. No newest, no top-rated, no best-selling.
  • Recommended Age is seller-typed free text, so its most valuable facet is unusable.
  • Its product page emits Product with no offers, no aggregateRating and an empty BreadcrumbList.
  • No GA4, GTM or Meta Pixel — Alibaba's first-party stack only. No hreflang despite bilingual content.
  • Bangla is visibly machine-translated: underscores leak into facet titles, and Interest is rendered সুদ — financial interest. Navigation shows ছাগল (goat).
  • Returns and payment pages serve one identical 149 KB empty shell; the Help Centre's body text is literally Help Center. Terms are unreadable.

Othoba — kids' toys

Also nopCommerce. Five category levels, with toys filed oddly under FMCG › Mother & Baby › Toys. Its product-page delivery block is the best single element found in the whole audit: delivery area, a location selector, separate inside- and outside-Dhaka timeframes, the shipping charge, and COD availability, all in one place.

Where it is weak

  • Assortment is effectively empty — /kids-toys returns one product; /soft-toy and /education-learning return none.
  • Only three facets (brand, vendor, rating) and no price, age or gender filter. Sort options ship with raw nopCommerce enum names as visible labels: NameAsc, PriceDesc, NewArrivals.
  • No guest checkout — phone plus OTP is mandatory before buying.
  • Returns are inspect-at-the-door: “After you accept the package, we are unable to receive claims for missing, wrong or defective items.” An effectively zero-day window.
  • Tracking requires logging in; the footer's Track My Order link is href="#". Zero Open Graph tags and a 0-byte sitemap.

Where a specialist beats a marketplace

Age and developmental stage as a first-class, structured, combinable axis — which Daraz cannot do because its age data is seller-typed. Curation and safety provenance in a category where quality is unverifiable. Consultative pre-purchase help. Occasion merchandising for birthdays and Eid, which neither marketplace offers. Content authority, since every niche blog is empty. Cross-SKU developmental bundles, which a horizontal marketplace structurally cannot assemble. A frictionless guest COD checkout, which is an edge over both marketplaces — they each gate identity. And hand-written Bangla, against Daraz's machine translation.

09

The five most exploitable weaknesses

  1. Structured data and Bangla-first SEO are unowned

    Krystal has 820 products and emits zero JSON-LD, zero canonical and zero Open Graph. Kids Zone BD emits nothing. SK Toy's Product schema is actively broken. Only one site has AggregateRating, built on one review. No site has hreflang or a bn-BD variant — including both marketplaces. Shipping correct Product + Offer + AggregateRating + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage with Bangla fields takes the rich results and the AI-answer citations uncontested — and three sites have additionally excluded themselves from AI crawlers.

  2. Checkout friction, with Toyshop as the extreme

    No COD, manual money transfer before an order exists, three steps, a required email, a math captcha, and untranslated address labels. Toy House BD is COD-only and collects no phone number. Krystal is the bar to beat — and even it has no cart page and contradicts its own shipping policy. A one-page, phone-first, district-to-thana, COD-default checkout with the charge already known beats seven of nine outright.

  3. Two competitors have conceded organic search

    Kids Zone BD serves one identical shell for every URL, has no sitemap, runs no analytics of any kind, and does not appear in a search for its own name. SK Toy server-renders its homepage but ships a spinning loader on every product and category page. Those queries are free.

  4. Stock, price and policy data are visibly wrong across the board

    Products at ৳0 marked in stock. 19 of 28 homepage products unavailable. Three contradictory delivery thresholds, on three separate sites. A hotline printed as xxxx-xxxxx. A product whose age badge and specifications disagree with each other. Live gambling and essay-mill spam pages on a children's store. Accurate stock, real prices and one consistent policy are low-cost and high-trust.

  5. The toy-specific merchandising a parent needs does not exist

    No combinable age, brand or gender faceting anywhere. Bundles on one site of nine. No countdown mechanics in the entire niche — one competitor has the capability and switched it off. No gift flows on any of the eleven properties. No structured safety guidance. Search that breaks on a one-letter typo. One review across eleven hundred products.

10

What this means for the build

The full requirement set, with IDs and MVP priority, is in docs/PRODUCT-REQUIREMENTS.md. The gap analysis ranked by impact over effort is in docs/FEATURE-MATRIX.md.

Storefront

  • Age in months, plus skill, brand, gender and price — combinable, URL-addressable and therefore indexable.
  • Typo-tolerant Bangla ↔ Banglish ↔ English search.
  • Accurate per-variant stock.
  • The delivery charge on the product page, from the same code path as checkout, so the two can never disagree.
  • One-page, phone-first, COD-default checkout with district → upazila → area and a landmark field.
  • Moderated reviews with verified-purchase proof and AggregateRating.
  • Systematic safety and certification information.
  • Named courier with a real shipment timeline. Flash sales with a countdown. Complete schema including FAQPage. Automatic 301s from slug history. A blog that has posts.

Back office — where no competitor competes

  • Append-only stock ledger; suppliers and purchase orders with landed cost.
  • Pathao and Steadfast one-click and bulk booking behind one abstraction, with per-provider location mapping and a failed-booking retry queue.
  • Partial delivery handled — both couriers support it; AppByte has no such state.
  • Phone-keyed risk screening with a visible score breakdown, and official courier data shown as a separate, timestamped panel rather than blended into one number.
  • COD settlement reconciliation matching remittances to consignments.
  • A P&L that keeps revenue, cash collected and receivables separate, and nets off courier charges, COD fees, return charges, gateway fees, packaging, discounts, refunds and write-offs.

Two things outside the software that need attention first

The supplied Figma file is empty — one page, zero nodes, no styles, no variables, still named “Document”. There was nothing to review, so the design system was written from first principles instead. Design work cannot start until this is resolved.

Toys came under mandatory BSTI certification in 2026 — R.O. 232/233-Act/2026, gazetted 23 June 2026, effective 23 August 2026, covering the BDS ISO 8124 safety standards. This post-dates every compliance guide currently online, which is why it is easy to miss, and whether an importer needs a full CM licence or consignment-wise port clearance could not be resolved from public sources.
